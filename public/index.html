UNRIVALLED WILDERNESS

Explore the Wild Heart of Tanzania

Discover legendary landscapes, untamed wilderness, and extraordinary wildlife across Tanzania’s most remarkable safari destinations.

Endless Plains

Serengeti National Park

Vast plains, extraordinary wildlife and some of Africa's most unforgettable natural spectacles. Serengeti is the crown jewel of African wilderness, hosting over two million wildebeest and zebras on their eternal migration journey.

Explore Serengeti →
The Eighth Wonder of the World

Ngorongoro Crater

Descend into one of Africa's most remarkable wildlife destinations, where dramatic landscapes surround an extraordinary concentration of wildlife. A pristine 600m deep volcanic caldera housing over 25,000 large animals.

Explore Ngorongoro →
Land of Giants, Ancient Baobabs

Tarangire National Park

Known for its magnificent elephants, ancient baobabs and distinctive landscapes, Tarangire offers a quieter and deeply rewarding safari experience along the scenic Tarangire River.

Explore Tarangire →
Rift Valley Jewel

Lake Manyara National Park

A compact and diverse ecosystem where forests, wetlands, open plains and wildlife come together beneath the dramatic Rift Valley landscape, famous for tree-climbing lions and flamingo flocks.

Explore Lake →
Roof of Africa

Mount Kilimanjaro

Experience the presence of Africa's highest mountain (5,895m), whether as the centerpiece of your journey with a summit expedition or as part of a scenic wider Tanzanian adventure.

Explore Mount →
Turquoise Waters, Spice Island

Zanzibar Archipelago

Complete your African journey with warm Indian Ocean waters, rich history, beautiful beaches and the distinctive character of Zanzibar. The perfect bush-to-beach finale.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning your Tanzanian safari adventure.

How do I book a safari with Malunda Explorers?

Booking is simple and transparent. You can choose any of our featured safari packages or use our interactive booking system to configure your dates, travelers, and accommodation. Once you submit, you receive an immediate booking reference (e.g., ME-2026-0001) and our safari director confirms availability within a few hours. To lock in lodge bookings, a 30% deposit is payable online or via wire transfer.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. A 30% deposit is required at the time of confirmation to secure high-demand national park permits, luxury tented camp rooms, and private 4x4 vehicles. The remaining balance of 70% is due 30 days prior to your safari start date, or upon arrival in Arusha by prior arrangement.

What payment methods are available?

We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) via secure encrypted gateway, international direct bank wire transfers in USD or EUR to our CRDB Bank account in Arusha, and East African mobile money (M-Pesa, Airtel Money).

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made more than 60 days before safari departure receive a 90% refund of total deposits (10% retained for unrecoverable park and administrative costs). Cancellations between 30 and 59 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations under 30 days are non-refundable as lodges have locked in bookings, though we always assist in rescheduling where possible.

Can I customize my safari itinerary?

Absolutely! Tailor-made journeys are our specialty. You can adjust the number of days, swap destinations, alter accommodation tiers (budget, mid-range, or 5-star luxury), and add extensions like Zanzibar or Mount Kilimanjaro.
