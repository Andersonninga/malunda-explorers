Safari Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about packing, permits, payments, game drives, best seasons, and health considerations.

How do I book a safari with Malunda Explorers?

Booking is simple and transparent. You can choose any of our featured safari packages or use our interactive booking system to configure your dates, travelers, and accommodation. Once you submit, you receive an immediate booking reference (e.g., ME-2026-0001) and our safari director confirms availability within a few hours. To lock in lodge bookings, a 30% deposit is payable online or via wire transfer.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. A 30% deposit is required at the time of confirmation to secure high-demand national park permits, luxury tented camp rooms, and private 4x4 vehicles. The remaining balance of 70% is due 30 days prior to your safari start date, or upon arrival in Arusha by prior arrangement.

What payment methods are available?

We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) via secure encrypted gateway, international direct bank wire transfers in USD or EUR to our CRDB Bank account in Arusha, and East African mobile money (M-Pesa, Airtel Money).

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made more than 60 days before safari departure receive a 90% refund of total deposits (10% retained for unrecoverable park and administrative costs). Cancellations between 30 and 59 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations under 30 days are non-refundable as lodges have locked in bookings, though we always assist in rescheduling where possible.

Can I customize my safari itinerary?

Absolutely! Tailor-made journeys are our specialty. You can adjust the number of days, swap destinations, alter accommodation tiers (budget, mid-range, or 5-star luxury), and add extensions like Zanzibar or Mount Kilimanjaro.

What is included in a Malunda Explorers safari?

Our safaris are all-inclusive on the ground: all Tanzania National Park entrance fees, conservation fees, concession fees, crater service permits, private 4x4 Land Cruiser with pop-up roof and English-speaking guide, all meals (breakfast, lunch/picnic, dinner), unlimited chilled bottled water in the vehicle, all lodge accommodations, and AMREF Flying Doctors emergency medical evacuation insurance.

What is not included in the safari price?

International airfare to Tanzania, Tanzania entry visa fees ($50 for most nationalities, $100 for US citizens), personal travel and health insurance, alcoholic beverages and personal laundry at lodges (unless booked under Ultra-Luxury tier), gratuities for your guide and lodge staff, and optional activities like hot air balloon rides.

What is the best time to visit Tanzania for a safari?

Tanzania is a premier year-round safari destination. The dry season from late June to October offers prime wildlife viewing around water sources and river crossings of the Great Migration. January to March is fantastic in the southern Serengeti for the wildebeest calving season with high predator action. The short rains in November-December and long rains in April-May offer lush emerald scenery and fewer crowds.

Is Tanzania suitable for family safaris with children?

Yes, Tanzania is exceptionally warm and welcoming to families. We recommend children be 5 years or older to comfortably enjoy game drives. Our family safaris feature interconnected rooms, swimming pools, flexible schedules, and engaging cultural activities like archery with the Hadzabe.

Can you arrange airport transfers and Zanzibar extensions?

Yes, we handle seamless transfers between Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), Arusha Airport (ARK), and local hotels. We also organize domestic bush flights directly from Serengeti airstrips to Zanzibar for stress-free bush-to-beach vacations.

What happens after I book?

Immediately after submitting your booking, you receive a confirmation email with your booking reference. Within 12-24 hours, our safari operations team sends you a personalized welcome pack containing your day-by-day itinerary, lodge confirmations, Tanzania packing list, visa advice, and direct WhatsApp contact with your dedicated safari coordinator.
