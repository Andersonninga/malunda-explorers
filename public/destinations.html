Tanzania Safari Destinations

Discover legendary national parks, untamed wildlife sanctuaries, ancient craters, and tropical Indian Ocean islands across Tanzania’s famed Northern Circuit and coastline.

DESTINATION GUIDE

Serengeti National Park

Endless Plains & The Great Wildebeest Migration

Vast plains, extraordinary wildlife and some of Africa's most unforgettable natural spectacles. Serengeti is the crown jewel of African wilderness, hosting over two million wildebeest and zebras on their eternal migration journey.

Best Time to Visit
Year-round; River crossings (July-October), Calving season (January-March)
Recommended Duration
3 - 5 Days
Key Wildlife Highlights:
Big Five (Lion, Leopard, Elephant, Buffalo, Rhino)Great Wildebeest MigrationCheetah coalitionsNile Crocodiles in Mara RiverOver 500 bird species

Ngorongoro Crater

The Eighth Wonder of the World & Wildlife Sanctuary

Descend into one of Africa's most remarkable wildlife destinations, where dramatic landscapes surround an extraordinary concentration of wildlife. A pristine 600m deep volcanic caldera housing over 25,000 large animals.

Best Time to Visit
Excellent year-round; dry season (June-October) has easiest wildlife viewing
Recommended Duration
1 - 2 Days
Key Wildlife Highlights:
Endangered Black RhinoDense pride of lionsGiant bull tuskersThousands of pink flamingos on Lake MagadiSpotted hyenas

Tarangire National Park

Land of Giants, Ancient Baobabs & Elephant Herds

Known for its magnificent elephants, ancient baobabs and distinctive landscapes, Tarangire offers a quieter and deeply rewarding safari experience along the scenic Tarangire River.

Best Time to Visit
July to October (Dry season river congregation)
Recommended Duration
1 - 2 Days
Key Wildlife Highlights:
Enormous elephant herds (up to 300 individuals)Ancient thousand-year-old baobabsTree-climbing pythonsOryx and gerenukOver 550 bird species

Lake Manyara National Park

Rift Valley Jewel & Tree-Climbing Lions

A compact and diverse ecosystem where forests, wetlands, open plains and wildlife come together beneath the dramatic Rift Valley landscape, famous for tree-climbing lions and flamingo flocks.

Best Time to Visit
Dry season (June-October) for mammals; Wet season (November-May) for flamingos and birds
Recommended Duration
1 Day
Key Wildlife Highlights:
Legendary tree-climbing lionsMassive baboon troopsLesser and Greater FlamingosHippos in hippo poolElephants in groundwater forest

Mount Kilimanjaro

Roof of Africa & Highest Free-Standing Mountain

Experience the presence of Africa's highest mountain (5,895m), whether as the centerpiece of your journey with a summit expedition or as part of a scenic wider Tanzanian adventure.

Best Time to Visit
January-March and June-October (clearest skies and warmest conditions)
Recommended Duration
6 - 8 Days for summit; 1 Day for scenic foothills hike
Key Wildlife Highlights:
Blue monkeys & colobus in rainforestAlpine vegetationGiant groundsels and lobeliasKlipspringersBirdlife

Zanzibar Archipelago

Turquoise Waters, Spice Island & Stone Town Heritage

Complete your African journey with warm Indian Ocean waters, rich history, beautiful beaches and the distinctive character of Zanzibar. The perfect bush-to-beach finale.

Best Time to Visit
June to October and December to February (dry and sunny beach weather)
Recommended Duration
3 - 5 Days
Key Wildlife Highlights:
Bottlenose dolphins at KizimkaziGreen turtles at Mnemba AtollRed Colobus Monkeys in Jozani ForestVibrant coral reef marine life
